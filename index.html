FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers, no fine print.

What does Winnipeg Web Works actually do?+

We build and fix websites, and run the ongoing marketing (SEO, social, ads) that keeps a business showing up online. We start every relationship with a free audit so you see exactly what’s broken before you spend anything.

Is the audit actually free?+

Yes. No cost and no card required. The 15-minute call and the written report are free, whether or not you ever work with us again.

What do engagements cost?+

Paid engagements start at $10,000. The exact scope and price are set after your free audit, based on what it turns up — so you only ever pay to fix real, measured leaks.

Do I need to sign anything or give payment info for the audit?+

No. Just your name and email to book the call. No contract, no card.

What exactly do you look at?+

Your website (speed, mobile experience, dead ends), your ads if you’re running any (wasted spend, wrong targeting), and your Google visibility (search ranking, reviews, and whether you show up for “near me” searches).

What’s the Digital Facelift, and how’s it different from a full rebuild?+

Some sites (and internal tools like dashboards or admin panels) work fine — they just look unfinished, and that costs you trust before a customer reads a word. The Facelift is a visual overhaul: same pages, same features, same backend, rebuilt to look like a business people want to pay more. If your audit turns up real functional problems, we’ll tell you honestly and point you at the Signature Website instead.
